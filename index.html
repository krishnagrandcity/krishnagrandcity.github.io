Our Society

A Thriving Community Since 2018

Krishna Grand City Society is home to over 247 residents across 94 units spread across 2 BHK apartments, 3 BHK apartments, and independent row houses in Sheetala Colony, Kohka, Bhilai.

Our elected Resident Welfare Association (RWA) manages day-to-day administration, facility upkeep, security and financial planning — all with full transparency to residents.

This digital portal was created to modernise our record-keeping, making it easier for committee members to serve the community effectively.

🏛️ The Club House

Krishna Grand City's Club House is the social heart of our community — a premium, multi-purpose complex designed to enrich the lifestyle of every resident. Spanning over 8,000 sq. ft., the Club House brings together recreational, fitness, and celebration spaces under one roof. Whether you are looking to stay fit, host a family event, or simply unwind with neighbours, the Club House has it all.

🏋️

The Gymnasium

Located on the ground floor of the Club House

Our state-of-the-art gymnasium spans 2,200 sq. ft. and is equipped with the latest cardio and strength-training equipment from reputed brands. The gym features 10 treadmills, 8 elliptical machines, a dedicated free-weight zone, resistance machines, and a stretching/yoga corner. Two full-wall mirrors and premium rubber flooring ensure a professional workout experience. Certified personal trainers are available Monday through Saturday from 6 AM to 8 PM, offering both group classes (Zumba, aerobics, yoga) and one-on-one sessions. The gym is open 365 days a year, including public holidays, ensuring residents can maintain their fitness routines uninterrupted.

The Community Hall

First floor of the Club House · Capacity: 250 persons

The Community Hall is a versatile, air-conditioned event space spanning 3,500 sq. ft. on the first floor of the Club House. Designed to serve as the venue for all of the society's social, cultural, and celebratory occasions, the hall features a raised stage with sound and lighting systems, a retractable partition system that divides the space into two independent halls when required, and a fully equipped catering kitchen adjacent to the main hall. Premium stackable chairs and folding banquet tables accommodate up to 250 guests in a banquet setup or 350 in a theatre-style arrangement. The hall is available for booking by all registered residents for occasions such as birthday parties, anniversaries, Pooja ceremonies, kitty parties, society meetings, and cultural programs. A dedicated in-house audio-visual team is available on request.
