Krishna Grand City
Our Society

A Thriving Community Since 2018

Krishna Grand City Society is home to over 247 residents across 94 units spread across 2 BHK apartments, 3 BHK apartments, and independent row houses in Sheetala Colony, Kohka, Bhilai.

Our elected Resident Welfare Association (RWA) manages day-to-day administration, facility upkeep, security and financial planning — all with full transparency to residents.

This digital portal was created to modernise our record-keeping, making it easier for committee members to serve the community effectively.
